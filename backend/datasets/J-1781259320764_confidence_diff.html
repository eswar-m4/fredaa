    HIGHLIGHTS

    The Keysight 10020A Resistive Divider Probe Kit is a signal probing system for measuring fast-transition-time signals in high-impedance systems. It is designed for use with 50 Ω input oscilloscopes, but may be used with other than 50 Ω systems if a 50 Ω feedthrough termination such as the Keysight 10100C, is used.

    The Keysight 10240B Blocking Capacitor is a probing accessessory that provides ac-coupling while maintaining a 50 ohm system. This capacitor is designed for use with the model 10020A Resistive Divider Kit, but may be used with any probe that must be terminated in 50 ohms.

    The Keysight 10229A Hook Tip Adapter is an available accessory.

    • Low input RC provides high-fidelity probing of fast-transition signals
    • The Keysight 10218A BNC Adapter allows the Resistive Divider to be connected to a BNC connector
    • Use of the Keysight 10240B Blocking Capacitor provides ac-coupling at the divider output
    • A spanner tip provides a very short ground lead, which reduces ringing in high-frequency probing
    • A special probe handle fits over the short vinyl tip for convenient use
    • Output Connector: BNC
    • Length (overall): Approximately 1.2 m (4 ft)
    • Weight: Net 0.45 kg (1 lb)
